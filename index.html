Simple training method

1. Listen once

Play the clip once without looking at the transcript. Do not pause unless the real test allows it.

2. Write or summarise

For exact-word practice, write as much as possible. For interview-style practice, write the main message in one clear sentence.

3. Compare and repeat

Reveal the transcript. Note the missed sounds, words or rhythm. Repeat the clip aloud once or twice.

What to track

  • Which accents are hardest?
  • Are numbers, names or short function words being missed?
  • Does speed or rhythm cause more difficulty than vocabulary?
  • Can he summarise the message after one listen?

Short Spanish cue

No traduzcas palabra por palabra. Primero entiende la idea principal; después revisa los sonidos concretos que fallaron.
